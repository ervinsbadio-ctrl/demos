## What he said on the call, in his words
- "It's not done. I don't like the blue."

## Positioning change (the important one)
Badio Digital is no longer "salon websites". It is a small digital studio for local businesses selling three
things: a website, an AI receptionist that answers the phone, and a booking flow. Salons stay as the proof
(Lavalette is real and he built it), not as the limit.

## Art direction card
- FEELING: dark, sharp, built by a designer.
- SIGNATURE IDEA: a thin red-orange rule that runs through the page and becomes the accent on every button.
- PALETTE (no blue anywhere, do not reuse navy #17324D):
  ground #08090A / ground-alt #0E1011 / surface #141618 / ink #F3EFE7 / ink-muted #9A948B /
  accent #FF4A1C / line rgba(243,239,231,0.10)
- TYPE: Archivo 700/800 for every heading, tracking -0.03em, line-height 0.95. Inter 400/500 body at 17px / 1.65.
  Eyebrow: Archivo 600, 11px, uppercase, tracking 0.18em, ink-muted.
  Desktop h1 clamp 48-84px, h2 40px, h3 20px. Phone h1 38px, h2 28px.
- SHAPE: cards 16px radius, buttons fully rounded pill, images 12px radius, 1px hairline borders.
- GRID: max 1180px, gutters 20px phone / 48px desktop, section padding 72px phone / 112px desktop.
- TEXTURE: one fixed faint 32px grid plus two soft accent glow blobs behind the hero at opacity 0.10, blur 120px.
  Nothing else decorative.

## Motion (this is what he asked for)
- Reveal on scroll: sections fade up 16px over 500ms, 80ms stagger per child, IntersectionObserver, once.
- Hero headline: words rise in 60ms apart on load.
- Header: transparent at top, then ground at 80% with backdrop blur and a 1px bottom line after 40px scrolled.
- Buttons lift 2px with an accent glow on hover, 160ms. Cards lift 4px and their border goes accent at 25%.
- One marquee strip under the hero: "Websites / AI receptionist / Booking / Branding / Walkthroughs", slow,
  pauses on hover, track duplicated so it loops with no gap.
- Hard rules: nothing delays the hero copy or the button. All motion off under prefers-reduced-motion.
  Nothing moves more than 16px. No parallax. No autoplay video.
