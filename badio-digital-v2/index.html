Questions

The ones I get asked.

I don't need a website, everyone finds me on Instagram.

Instagram keeps the clients who already know you. A website is what shows up when someone who has never heard of you searches your business.

How do I know it will be any good?

You do not decide blind. You see the finished page first, with your number on it, and if it is not right when you look at it, you tell me and I change it.

Someone built me one before and it went nowhere.

Then be careful, and ask what he actually gave you. Here you see the page before it goes live, with your number on it so you can call it and watch it ring.

I don't have time to deal with this.

I need your services, your hours and a few photos, once, from your phone. If photos are the hard part, I pull them off your Facebook. If the phone is the hard part, the AI receptionist answers it for you.

What happens after the page goes live?

I keep the page running, changing your hours when they change, and fixing anything that breaks. You stop having to think about it.
